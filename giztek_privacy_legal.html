Privacy Policy

NanoGami does not collect or store personal user data. The application allows users to import, export, and work with various DNA-related file formats including JSON, PDB, .oxdna, .top, and .dat files. All files are accessed directly from the user’s device and no files are uploaded or stored externally by GizTek.

Third-Party Services

  • Google AdMob – used for displaying ads in the unpaid version of the app
  • Google Analytics – used only for aggregate usage and monetization reporting (no personal data is collected)
  • Firebase – may be integrated in the future for improved performance and app logging
  • Three.js and OrbitControls – used for interactive 3D visualization
